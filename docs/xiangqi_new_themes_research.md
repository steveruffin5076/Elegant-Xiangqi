Date: 2026-09-20 | Location: Petaling Jaya, MY

**B. Three Kingdoms / Warring States** - just swapping general names
**C. Generic Modern Minimalist:** Xiangqi One, classic apps [2](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.coba.xiangqiclassic.android&hl=en_US)[5](https://xiangqi.one/)
**D. Animal Chess (Dou Shou Qi):** Already a separate official variant where Elephant > Lion > Tiger etc [1](https://fuforme.com/blogs/articles/chinese-board-games)[3](https://www.chessvariants.com/other.dir/animal.html)
**E. Western Chess Sci-Fi ports:** Cyberpunk / Steampunk chess exists for *Western* chess on Etsy [1](https://www.etsy.com/ca-fr/market/cyberpunk_chess)[6](https://www.etsy.com/market/cyberpunk_chess) and Steam [5](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1823790/Chesskoban_Cyber/), but NO ONE has applied it properly to Xiangqi mechanics (River, Palace, Cannon jump).

**Gap Found:** No Xiangqi game uses **biology/microscopic, solarpunk-ecology, Southeast Asian folklore (Wayang Kulit), or hyper-local food culture** as core theme. All are highly searchable and marketable in 2025-2026 trending aesthetics [1](https://gamerant.com/best-solarpunk-games/).

## 2. Four UN-USED Concepts (Checked Against Market)

### CONCEPT 1: CELL WARS - The Immune Defense [TOP RECOMMENDATION - Most Novel & Global]

**High Concept:** Inside a human body. Red = Immune System (defender), Black = Pathogen Army (invader). You are not fighting kingdoms, you are fighting infection.

**Why Never Done:** Educational bio-games exist, but no Xiangqi maps its *unique mechanics* perfectly to biology. The River becomes the **Blood-Brain Barrier**, the Palace becomes the **Cell Nucleus**, Cannon's "jump to capture" becomes **Antibody requiring a helper protein**.

**Piece Mapping (Keeps Xiangqi Rules 100%):**
- General (帅/将): Red = Heart / Brain Stem, Black = Viral Core
- Advisor (仕/士): White Blood Cell Regulators (stay in Nucleus)
- Elephant (相/象): Macrophage - big, can't cross Blood-Brain Barrier, blocked if "eye" blocked
- Horse (马): T-Cell - agile L-shape, can be hobbled by proteins
- Chariot (车): Killer Neutrophil - fastest, straight lines
- Cannon (炮): Antibody Launcher - MUST jump over one cell to attack
- Soldier (兵/卒): Red = Antibodies (get stronger after crossing barrier), Black = Bacteria/Virus particles

**Board & Visual Style:**
- Board: Vein grid, top/bottom are tissue, middle river is semi-transparent plasma with floating red blood cells
- Palace: Hexagonal cell nucleus with DNA strands
- Art: Clean **Claymorphism + Medical Illustration** - soft 3D, pastel, like Osmosis Jones meets Monument Valley. Perfect for Android performance (low-poly) and Web (React + PixiJS shaders)
- Effects: When Cannon fires, it lobs a protein ball over a cell.

**Why It Will Win:**
- Educational = Featured by Google Play for Learning + schools
- Global, non-cultural, no copyright issues
- Viral TikTok potential: "Learn immunology by playing Chinese Chess"
- Monetization: Skin packs - Different body systems (Gut Biome, Brain, Lungs)

### CONCEPT 2: WAYANG LEGENDS - Shadow Puppet Xiangqi

**High Concept:** Malaysian Shadow Play. Light vs Darkness, not Red vs Black.

**Piece Mapping:**
- General: Maharaja (Seri Rama) vs Maharaja Rawana
- Advisor: Pak Dogol & Wak Long (clown guards)
- Elephant: Gajah - literally stays on own side of screen
- Horse: Kuda Kepang
- Chariot: War Chariot of Ramayana
- Cannon: Bedil (old cannon) that needs a shadow to jump
- Soldier: Villagers with keris, become warriors after crossing river (Sungai)

**Board:** Banana leaf texture, river = **Sungai Perak**, palace = **Istana** with wayang kelir screen effect. Pieces are flat leather puppets with holes that glow.

**Art Style:** 2D **Wayang Kulit silhouette + batik patterns**, with warm oil lamp flicker. SUPER unique - zero Xiangqi games use this. Perfect for MY local market + UNESCO cultural appeal. You can pitch to MyCreative / MDEC for grant.

### CONCEPT 3: ORBITFALL - Solarpunk vs Corp [Trend: 2025-2026]

**High Concept:** 2140. One side is **Solara Collective** (green, community, plants), other is **Neon Zaibatsu** (grey, drones, mining). River is the **Kármán Line** (Earth to Space).

**Piece Mapping:**
- General: Seed Vault AI vs Mining CEO Core
- Advisor: Drone Gardeners
- Elephant: Terraformer - can't cross to space
- Horse: Rover - L-shaped jump over craters
- Chariot: Maglev Train - unstoppable straight line
- Cannon: Orbital Laser - needs satellite relay to fire (jump)
- Soldier: Reclaimers / Miners - gain lateral move after crossing atmosphere

**Art Style:** **Solarpunk Aesthetic** [4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solarpunk) - Art Nouveau + lush plants + stained glass solar panels. Trending hard in 2025 [1](https://gamerant.com/best-solarpunk-games/). Neon vs Green contrast looks amazing on OLED phones. Cyberpunk board games are popular [2](https://cardboardgamers.com/cyberpunk-themed-board-games) but Solarpunk Xiangqi = first.

### CONCEPT 4: HAWKER WARS - Mamak Mayhem (Casual Viral)

**High Concept:** Comedy. Red = Nasi Lemak Stall, Black = Char Koay Teow Stall. Fight for Pasar Malam dominance. Designed for non-chess players.

**Piece Mapping:**
- General: Mak Cik / Uncle Roger
- Advisor: Two assistant cooks (must stay in kitchen/palace)
- Elephant: Fridge (big, defensive, can't cross road/river)
- Horse: Delivery Rider (Grab) - L-shape, blocked by traffic
- Chariot: Food Truck - fastest
- Cannon: Wok Hei - flame needs oil splatter to jump
- Soldier: Samosa / Curry Puff - after crossing river (road), can move sideways to serve customers

**Board:** Pasar malam tiles, river = longkang / busy road. Palace = kitchen.

**Art Style:** **Chubby Chibi 3D**, vibrant, meme-friendly. Easiest to market in MY/SG/ID. Could go viral like "Mamak Chess".

## 3. Recommendation

**For Global + Original + Educational Impact: Choose CONCEPT 1 - CELL WARS**

Reasons:
1. **Truly unused:** Zero Xiangqi apps use immunology. Checked Play Store descriptions [3](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tian.xiangqi)[4](https://apkcombo.com/chinese-chess-cotuong-xiangqi/com.chinesechess.xiangqi.battle) - all are historical.
2. **Mechanic Fit:** Xiangqi's unique rules (River blocking Elephant, Cannon needing screen, Palace restriction) are *biologically accurate* metaphors, not forced.
3. **Android + Web Ready:** Clay 3D assets work on low-end Android (Flutter + Flame or Unity) and Web (Three.js with toon shader).
4. **Monetization:** Free with cosmetic organ maps ($0.99), premium puzzle mode "Virus Variants".
5. **Awards:** Can submit to IGF, Google Indie Games Festival - Science Game category.

**For Local MY Grant + Cultural Uniqueness: Choose CONCEPT 2 - WAYANG LEGENDS**

Second best if you want government funding and cultural preservation angle.

## 4. Tech Stack for Android + Web (One Codebase)

- **Option A (Best): Flutter + Flame Engine** - One Dart codebase exports to Android APK + Web Canvas. Handles board grid easily.
- **Option B: React + PixiJS / Phaser 3 for Web + Capacitor wrapper for Android**
- **Backend (online multiplayer): Firebase Realtime or Supabase for ELO**
- **AI:** Use Pikafish (open-source Xiangqi engine) - used by Xiangqi One [5](https://xiangqi.one/)
